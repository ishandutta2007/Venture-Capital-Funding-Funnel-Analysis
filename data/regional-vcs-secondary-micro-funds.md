## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways for Founders

1. **Thesis Over Brand:** Regional funds optimize for capital efficiency and local market capture rather than Silicon Valley-style blitzscaling.
2. **Local Ecosystem Leverage:** Co-investing alongside state or university matching funds can double check sizes while reducing overall equity dilution.
3. **Gateway to Tier-1 Follow-ons:** Strong regional funds frequently syndicates with global Series A leaders once unit economics are validated.
