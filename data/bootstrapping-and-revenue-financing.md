## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways for Founders

1. **Retain 100% Equity:** Revenue-based and calm capital models allow founders to maintain controlling stakes and avoid forced exit timelines.
2. **Alignment with Cash Flow:** Repayment installments scale down during slower months and up during peak cycles, mitigating default risk.
3. **Qualification Requirements:** Unlike pre-seed VC which invests on a pitch deck, RBF requires at least $10K–$25K in demonstrable monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
