## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways for Founders

1. **Velocity and SPVs:** Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) via AngelList or Vauban allow lead angels to pool hundreds of micro-investors into a single clean line on the cap table.
2. **Operator Value-Add:** Unlike institutional funds, operator angels can offer immediate tactical execution help (e.g., enterprise sales intros, hiring engineers, architecture design).
3. **Warm Introductions Trump Cold Portals:** Syndicates rely heavily on referrals from other founders in their portfolio.
