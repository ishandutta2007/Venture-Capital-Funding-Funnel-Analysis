## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways for Founders

1. **Non-Dilutive Cap Table Protection:** Government grants (like SBIR or Horizon Europe) take 0% equity, preserving ownership for founders before commercial inflection.
2. **Community Validation via Crowdfunding:** A strong Reg CF campaign acts as marketing, customer acquisition, and pre-sales traction rolled into one.
3. **Patience Required:** Federal grant applications often require 6 to 9 months from submission to disbursement; founders must manage runway accordingly.
